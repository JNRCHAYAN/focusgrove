Privacy Policy — FocusGrove

Last updated: 13 September 2026

FocusGrove does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. The app has no account system, no login, and no server backend.

What the app stores, and where

Everything FocusGrove creates — your focus sessions, tree and forest history, coin balance, and settings — is stored only in local storage on your device, in a SQLite database and in Android SharedPreferences. None of it is ever uploaded anywhere. We operate no servers and hold no copy of your data.

Because there is no copy on our side, uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it. That is the only way to delete it, and it is complete.

Permissions

FocusGrove requests exactly one Android permission: notifications (Android 13 and later). It is used solely to alert you when a focus session or a break ends. The app asks for it the first time a session completes, not on first launch, and the app remains fully usable if you decline.

The notification permission does not grant access to any of your data, and FocusGrove requests no other permission — no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no storage, no internet.

No tracking of any kind

The app contains:

  • no advertising SDK
  • no analytics SDK
  • no crash-reporting SDK
  • no social or third-party SDKs

There are no trackers, no device identifiers, no advertising IDs, and no telemetry. FocusGrove works entirely offline.

Children

FocusGrove collects no data from anyone, including children. It is suitable for a general audience.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of FocusGrove ever changes how it handles data — it is not planned — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Because the app collects nothing today, any change would be visible here first.
